# Penguin Couple — Feature Audit and Store Screenshot Plan

Date: 28 July 2026

## Product position

Penguin Couple is a private shared space for two people. Its strongest promise is not any single utility; it is the combination of small daily interactions that help a couple feel present in each other's life:

- share a mood;
- answer a daily ritual or deeper question;
- draw or chat together in real time;
- play partner games;
- save memories and special dates;
- keep shared information visible through widgets;
- send a current photo or start a video call.

The store screenshots should sell this emotional outcome first and explain individual features second.

Recommended positioning line:

> Your little world for two.

Supporting description:

> Share moods, answer questions, draw, play, call, and save your story together.

## Recommended screenshot story

Use eight portrait screenshots. The first three matter most because stores frequently expose them before the user scrolls.

| Order | Headline | Supporting line | App screen and exact state |
|---:|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Your little world for two** | Share moods and everyday moments in one private space. | Home screen, top section. Both penguins visible in a positive mood combination, partner online, and no tooltips or offer banners. |
| 2 | **2,000+ questions for two** | Explore every side of your relationship. | Question-category or question-set library. Prioritize Relationship, Get To Know, Couple Therapy, Travel, and Future. |
| 3 | **Answer. Compare. Connect.** | Discover what your partner really thinks. | An interactive question card or completed answer comparison with both avatars visible. |
| 4 | **Draw together, live** | A shared canvas, wherever you are. | Draw Together live canvas with a two-colour doodle, “Live On,” and partner presence. Avoid showing the free-tier countdown. |
| 5 | **Playful challenges for two** | Jigsaw, Tic-Tac-Toe, and Wordle with your favourite opponent. | Games hub. Show all three game cards and make Tic-Tac-Toe or Wordle the active “Your turn” game. |
| 6 | **Keep your story together** | Save photos, special dates, and the moments you never want to forget. | Populated Our Timeline with three visually distinct entries. |
| 7 | **Love on your home screen** | See your days, distance, doodles, and latest photo at a glance. | Show the actual platform result: iOS lock/home widgets or Android home widgets. |
| 8 | **Video chat, just for two** | See, talk, and stay close from anywhere. | Connected Video Chat with both partners visible, camera enabled, controls unobstructed, and no permission or free-limit prompts. Use synthetic or fully consented faces. |

### Optional ninth and tenth frames

If ten iOS screenshots are desired:

9. **A mood only you two understand** — Mood picker or a second home mascot combination.
10. **Send a moment, just for them** — Couple Photo preview or partner-photo widget.

For Google Play, stay with eight because that is the maximum for a phone listing.

## Capture data that should be prepared first

Create two dedicated store-demo users. Do not use developer or customer accounts.

- Names: short, globally understandable names such as `Maya` and `Alex`.
- Avatars: warm, coordinated, synthetic or fully licensed images.
- Relationship age: around 300–800 days so the widget and home experience feel established.
- Moods: use a visually positive combination such as Relaxed + Cuddly or Excited + Relaxed.
- Presence: partner online for home, games, live drawing, and chat captures.
- Daily Ritual: one unanswered short question and one completed question for answer comparison.
- Topic sets: populate Relationship, Get To Know, Travel, and Future. Avoid Sex & Love and Naughty in store artwork.
- Timeline: at least five entries with a mix of memory and special-date cards. Use synthetic travel, coffee, landscape, and celebration images.
- Games: one pending Jigsaw, one mid-game Tic-Tac-Toe board, and one Wordle with two completed guesses.
- Chats: at least three question-derived threads with short, positive preview text.
- Canvas: prepare a simple, legible two-colour doodle that looks hand-drawn.
- Widgets: set the relationship date and enable demo distance data. Use platform-correct widget previews.
- Notifications: clear all badges except a deliberate single game-turn badge if it improves the game story.

## Visual production direction

- Keep the current pink–lilac–blue gradient language; it is distinctive and consistent with the app.
- Use a short headline above the phone UI, ideally five words or fewer.
- Keep the actual UI large. It should occupy roughly 65–75% of each artwork.
- Keep text and faces away from outer edges because store surfaces crop unpredictably.
- Use one typography system across all frames; the app's rounded, friendly weight is the right direction.
- Alternate layouts slightly so eight frames do not feel mechanically repeated, but keep the same background and device scale.
- Use actual in-app screens. Decorative hearts, glows, or mascots can support the composition but must not imply unavailable functionality.
- Do not place the app inside a generic device frame on Google Play; Google recommends avoiding device imagery that can become obsolete.
- Use a clean status bar: no carrier name, notifications, low battery, VPN, or developer indicators.
- Do not show permission dialogs, loading spinners, error messages, empty screens, premium-limit sheets, debug controls, or keyboard overlays.
- Do not advertise a price, discount, “7 Days Free,” ranking, award, or “best” claim in Google Play screenshots.

## Platform deliverables

### Apple App Store

- Apple accepts 1–10 screenshots in JPEG/JPG/PNG without transparency.
- Build the primary iPhone set for a supported 6.9-inch size. A practical target is **1320 × 2868 px portrait** when captured from the matching device; Apple also accepts the other listed 6.9-inch resolutions.
- If the same UI is used across device sizes and localizations, App Store Connect can scale the highest-resolution required set down.
- If the app is available on iPad, capture genuine iPad layouts rather than stretching phone artwork.

Official references:

- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/manage-app-information/upload-app-previews-and-screenshots
- https://developer.apple.com/help/app-store-connect/reference/app-information/screenshot-specifications/

### Google Play

- Google Play accepts up to **8 phone screenshots**.
- Export portrait artwork at **1080 × 1920 px (9:16)**. This meets Google's recommended format for screenshot-led discovery surfaces.
- At least two screenshots are required overall, while four or more 1080px screenshots are recommended for app discovery placements.
- Screenshots must be JPEG or 24-bit PNG without alpha and accurately show the current app.
- Localize any added headline or supporting copy for each language-specific listing.
- Also prepare a separate **1024 × 500 px** feature graphic; it is not a phone screenshot.

Official references:

- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/9866151
- https://support.google.com/googleplay/android-developer/answer/13393723

## Localization recommendation

The app currently supports seven UI languages, but producing seven complete store sets at once is expensive. Recommended rollout:

1. English master set.
2. Localize the overlay copy and recapture dynamic in-app text for the top target markets.
3. Start with Spanish, French, German, and Japanese if those markets match acquisition plans.
4. Add Italian and Korean after the master layout and copy have stabilized.

Do not put translated marketing text over an English in-app screen. Google explicitly recommends separate localized screenshot assets when screenshots contain text.

## Screens that should not lead the listing

- Login and permission screens explain setup, not value.
- Settings and account management are expected utilities, not differentiators.
- The premium paywall interrupts the emotional story and introduces promotional-policy risk.
- Notification Center is useful but visually weaker than the experiences it routes to.
- Empty Timeline, empty Chats, “partner offline,” loading, and free-limit states make the product feel inactive.
- Adult question categories should not appear in public listing artwork, particularly on Google Play.

## Capture checklist

Before each capture:

- use a release or release-like build with debug UI disabled;
- use seeded demo accounts and synthetic/consented content;
- verify both partners are paired and the required partner is online;
- set the correct app language;
- remove all permission prompts and tutorials;
- wait for all images, avatars, and fonts to finish loading;
- hide the keyboard unless it is essential to the feature;
- check that no personal names, photos, codes, chat messages, or locations are real;
- check the status bar and safe areas;
- capture iOS and Android separately where platform UI differs;
- compare the screenshot with the submitted build so every claim remains accurate.

## Final recommendation

The best-performing narrative for Penguin Couple is:

> Feel closer → talk more deeply → do things together → keep the relationship visible.

That means the first three frames should be Home, Daily Ritual, and Draw Together—not onboarding, login, or the games list. Games, Timeline, widgets, and calling then prove that Penguin Couple has enough depth to become a daily couple app rather than a single-feature novelty.
