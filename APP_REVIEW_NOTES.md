## Testing Instructions

### 1. Partner Linking Feature
- The demo account already has a partner linked
- To test fresh partner linking: Create a new account, tap "Link Partner" on the home screen, and use the unique invite code system
- Both users must share their invite codes with each other

### 2. Daily Challenges
- New challenges are available every day at midnight (user's local timezone)
- The demo account may have partially completed challenges; new challenges refresh daily
- Swipe through cards to answer different question types

### 3. Games (Duels)

**Jigsaw Puzzle:**
- Tap "Create & Share" to select a photo from your gallery
- Choose difficulty level and send to partner
- Partner receives notification and can solve the puzzle

**Tic Tac Toe:**
- Tap the Tic Tac Toe card to start a new game
- Games are turn-based with push notifications

**Wordle:**
- Set a 5-letter word for your partner to guess
- Partner has 6 attempts to guess the word

### 4. Canvas (Scribble)
- Draw using finger gestures on the canvas
- Select colors and brush sizes from the toolbar
- Tap "Send to Your Love" to share with partner
- Partner receives the drawing via push notification and widget

### 5. iOS Widget
- Long press on home screen → Tap "+" → Search "Penguin"
- Widget displays partner's latest scribble drawing
- Updates automatically when partner sends a new drawing

### 6. Premium Subscription
- Tap profile icon → "Upgrade to Premium"
- Use sandbox test accounts for in-app purchase testing
- Subscription provides: Unlimited Questions, All Games, Premium Topics, No Ads

## Features Requiring Two Devices

The following features require a linked partner (second device/account):
- All games (Jigsaw, Tic Tac Toe, Wordle)
- Canvas/Scribble sending
- Daily Challenge answer comparisons
- Mood sharing visibility

**Demo account is pre-linked** so you can test with a single device, but full functionality is demonstrated with two linked accounts.
